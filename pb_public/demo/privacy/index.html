Privacy & retention

The demo vault and your communication choices have different lifetimes.

What the service handles

The planned service processes your verified Google identity and email for authentication, demo enrollment and your optional communication preferences. It stores encrypted vault data and operational metadata. Names and file contents are encrypted by the client; the server does not receive your vault passphrase.

Proposed retention

Demo accounts and vault data
Reset daily at 00:00 UTC, including encrypted identity bundles, keys, file versions and stored ciphertext. The daily reset does not remove local downloads or independent exports.
Email without marketing consent
Retained separately for up to 30 days after your last demo use.
Consented marketing contact details and preferences
Retained separately from the demo vault, reviewed after 12 months, and removable on withdrawal subject to any necessary minimal suppression or consent-evidence obligations.
Operational logs
Retained for up to 30 days, with a limited documented investigation period for security incidents. Logs must exclude passphrases, authentication credentials and file contents.

Your choices

Commercial contact and newsletter consent are optional and separate. Declining both does not prevent demo access. A launched service must provide a working withdrawal mechanism and identify any limited data retained to honor an unsubscribe request.

Static preview

The static preview stores no enrollment choices, cookies, browser tokens or analytics data. Choices exist only in the current page. Loading a hosted page still involves ordinary web-host network processing; do not interpret this as a promise that the hosting provider receives no network metadata.

Before launch

Final controller identity, privacy contact, processors, international transfer details, applicable legal bases and rights request process must be published before real public enrollment. The policy above describes the intended implementation and is not evidence that the backend is deployed.
