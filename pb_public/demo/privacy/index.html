What the service handles

The public demo processes your verified Google identity and email for authentication, demo enrollment and your optional communication preferences. It stores encrypted vault data and operational metadata. Names and file contents are encrypted by the client; the server does not receive your vault passphrase.

Retention

Demo accounts and vault data
Reset daily at 00:00 UTC, including encrypted identity bundles, keys, file versions and stored ciphertext. The daily reset does not remove local downloads or independent exports.
Email without marketing consent
Retained separately for up to 30 days after your last demo use.
Consented marketing contact details and preferences
Retained separately from the demo vault, reviewed after 12 months, and removable on withdrawal subject to any necessary minimal suppression or consent-evidence obligations.
Operational logs
Retained for up to 30 days, with a limited documented investigation period for security incidents. Logs must exclude passphrases, authentication credentials and file contents.

Isolated recovery backups

Nonmarketing contact records and ordinary security logs are removed from the live service after their stated 30-day retention periods. Copies in isolated recovery snapshots may remain for up to an additional 30 days before expiry. These backups are restricted to recovery and are not available for ordinary marketing use. The additional 30-day limit applies to isolated recovery copies, which are checked separately from live records. It does not describe physical erasure of storage media held by providers. A documented security incident may extend the stated live-log investigation period.

Before restored contact data can be used for any commercial email or newsletter, withdrawals and deletion requests must be reconciled against the latest authoritative records. Restoration must not reactivate old marketing permissions. If current suppression and deletion state cannot be established, sending remains blocked.

This separate backup allowance applies to durable contact and security records only. Demo accounts, encrypted keys, files and their ephemeral replicas remain subject to the strict daily reset; the allowance does not retain demo vault history.

Browser sign-in

The connected demo stores your application authentication token and account record in this browser’s local storage so sign-in survives a reload. Sign out to remove them; open tabs synchronize sign-out. The page checks saved authentication with the server before using it and clears expired or rejected sessions and sessions present when it observes the daily reset. Closed browsers may retain an unusable token until the next visit or until you clear site data. Vault passphrases, private keys, file contents and withdrawal links are not stored there. Signing out of the website does not sign out your CLI.

Your choices

Commercial contact and newsletter consent are optional and separate. Declining both does not prevent demo access. After enrollment, use “Withdraw both marketing permissions” to withdraw both choices. A supplied private withdrawal link also lets you confirm withdrawal without Google sign-in. Minimal purpose-specific suppression records are retained separately to honor your withdrawal; signing in again or resetting the demo does not reverse it.

Separate static preview

If a separately served copy is labelled “Design preview,” it does not authenticate with Google or save enrollment choices, cookies, browser tokens or analytics data. Its simulated choices exist only in the current page. These preview limits do not describe the connected public demo. Loading a hosted page still involves ordinary web-host network processing; do not interpret this as a promise that the hosting provider receives no network metadata.
