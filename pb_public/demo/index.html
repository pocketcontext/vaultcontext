02Connect, then unlock +

Google identifies you; a separate passphrase unlocks your encryption keys. Enter it only in your private terminal. There are no recovery keys.

# Illustration only — .invalid is not a live service
export VAULTCONTEXT_URL=https://vault-demo.example.invalid
export VAULTCONTEXT_USER_EMAIL=demo@example.com
vaultcontext login --google
vaultcontext whoami
vaultcontext check
vaultcontext init
vaultcontext unlock --timeout 900
vaultcontext create 'Sample vault'

A future daily reset will require a fresh demo setup. Never enter passphrases in chat, command arguments or environment variables.

03Save, compare and restore +

Create a harmless sample. Replace the example IDs with those returned by a live client. Restore to a separate path.

vault_demo_dir=$(mktemp -d)
vault_demo_dir=$(cd "$vault_demo_dir" && pwd -P)
printf 'APP_ENV=demo\nAPI_TOKEN=not-a-secret\n' > "$vault_demo_dir/sample.env"
vaultcontext save VAULT_ID "$vault_demo_dir/sample.env"
vaultcontext compare DOCUMENT_ID "$vault_demo_dir/sample.env"
vaultcontext restore DOCUMENT_ID --to "$vault_demo_dir/restored.env"
vaultcontext lock

A server reset cannot erase files already restored to your computer. Review progress does not confirm that any CLI operation succeeded.

CLEAR FROM THE START

Thoughtful boundaries.

Proposed policies for the future demo.
This preview collects no enrollment data.

What am I agreeing to? +

The proposed demo terms permit evaluation with synthetic files only. Do not upload real credentials, confidential information or other people’s personal data. The demo is temporary, has no storage guarantee, and vault data resets at 00:00 UTC daily.

Sales contact and newsletter subscription are separate, optional choices. They are not required to try the demo. Final terms and controller details will be provided before any real enrollment.

What stays, and for how long? +
Vault data
Planned deletion every day at midnight UTC, including the demo’s encrypted files and keys.
Email without marketing consent
Proposed retention: 30 days after your last demo use.
Marketing preferences and contact details
Retained separately from vault data, with a review after 12 months and an option to withdraw consent. Minimal suppression records may remain to honor withdrawal.
Operational logs
Proposed retention: 30 days, without file content or passphrases; a documented security incident may require a limited investigation period.

The launched service must publish the full retention policy, withdrawal mechanism and any necessary consent-evidence exceptions. Here, nothing is submitted or saved; a refresh clears the interface choices.

What does the daily reset mean? +

The future demo will clear its accounts, encrypted keys and vault files daily, so you will start fresh after a reset. You may have less than 24 hours until the next reset. The countdown above is illustrative and uses your device clock; it does not report a running server job.

Files you download or restore, independent exports, and local client state are not erased by a server reset. Marketing records have their own retention policy.

Does Google sign-in unlock my files? +

No. Google authenticates your account. A separate passphrase protects your encryption identity. File contents and names are encrypted by the client before upload; account and operational metadata remain visible to the service.

Your execution host and unlocked agent are trusted. Losing your passphrase can mean losing access. There are no recovery keys.
