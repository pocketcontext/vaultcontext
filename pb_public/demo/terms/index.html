Evaluation only

Use synthetic or disposable sample files. Do not upload real credentials, confidential documents or personal data belonging to anyone else. Do not abuse the service, interfere with other accounts, or attempt to bypass access controls or limits.

Your account and your keys

The public demo accepts verified Google accounts, including Gmail and Workspace accounts, subject to organization restrictions. Google authenticates your account; your separate passphrase protects your encryption identity. There are no recovery keys. Your execution host and unlocked agent are trusted.

Daily reset and limits

Demo vault state is scheduled for deletion at 00:00 UTC each day. This is a fixed daily reset, not a guaranteed 24-hour trial. Demo accounts, encrypted identity bundles, vault keys, documents, versions and files are disposable. The service may pause during reset and has no durability or availability guarantee.

A server reset cannot erase copies, exports or files already restored on your own computer. Your email and communication preferences are stored separately from the daily reset and follow the privacy policy. Personal vaults only: sharing and passphrase changes are unavailable in the initial demo. The server enforces account, vault, document, version and storage quotas.

Optional communications

Commercial email and the PocketContext newsletter are independent, optional choices. You can try the demo without either. Consent does not authorize anyone to read your encrypted files or execute tasks. Enrollment saves your choices; it does not send commercial email or a newsletter. No email-sending service is connected to this demo.

Public demo and static preview

The public demo connects to Google and saves enrollment and communication preferences. A separately served static design preview is labelled “Design preview”; it uses a fictional identity and saves no account or preferences.

Review status

This is a public test service. The operator is identified above; final legal review of these test terms remains pending.
